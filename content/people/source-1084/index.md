Lu Jian, Ph.D. in Imaging Medicine and Nuclear Medicine, is an attending physician at the Interventional and Vascular Surgery Department of Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University, a best young scholar at Southeast University, a postgraduate tutor at the School of Medicine, and a young and middle-aged academic leader in Jiangsu Province’s “333 High-Level Talent Training Project.” Selected into the National High-Level Talent Program. Focusing on innovative areas of tumor treatment such as radioactive particle stents and interventional surgical robots, the research results have been recommended by 7 domestic and foreign medical guidelines. Main research directions: intelligent diagnosis and treatment of major diseases, research and development of intelligent interventional devices, and clinical research on artificial intelligence.

Hosted 6 national/provincial and ministerial level projects such as the National Key R&D Program Sub-Center Project, Jiangsu Provincial Key R&D Plan, and the National Natural Science Foundation Youth Fund. Has published 20 high-level papers in English as the first/corresponding author (including co-authors), with a cumulative impact factor of over 200 points. Including Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatology (cover article), Journal of Hepatology and other top medical journals. Authorized/applied for 14 patents.

He serves as the deputy director of the Particle Physics Expert Committee for Minimally Invasive Tumor Treatment of the Chinese Anti-Cancer Association and the deputy director of the Youth Committee of the Interventional Physician Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Doctors Association. He has successively won the Youth Academic Award from the three major interventional radiology societies in North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific, as well as the first prize of Jiangsu Province Science and Technology Award, the first prize of Jiangsu Province Medical New Technology Introduction Award and other scientific and technological awards.
