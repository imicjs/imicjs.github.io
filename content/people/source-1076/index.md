Zhu Haidong, MD, deputy chief physician, master's tutor, deputy director of Interventional and Vascular Surgery, Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University. He is currently an executive member of the Asia-Pacific Society of Interventional Radiology, a standing committee member and deputy director-general of the Interventional Physician Branch of the Chinese Medical Doctor Association, and the vice president of the Interventional Physician Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Doctor Association.

![Zhu Haidong](https://imic.nuist.edu.cn/__local/2/27/41/D73E7E2641222CFF102EC9F6523_3A8629C7_9C61.jpg)

Research directions include tumor intervention, musculoskeletal intervention, clinical research, interventional equipment R&D and transformation, etc. Long-term commitment to interventional clinical practice, clinical research, device development and transformation, etc. He has published nearly 30 SCI papers as the first author or co-first author in top magazines such as LANCET Oncology and Journal of Hepatology. He has hosted and is currently researching three National Natural Science Foundation projects, one Jiangsu Provincial Key Medical Talent Project, and has participated in a number of national, ministerial and provincial scientific research projects.
