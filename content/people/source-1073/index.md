Wang Hongxing, Ph.D., chief physician, doctoral supervisor, key young medical talent in Jiangsu Province, "333" young and middle-aged academic leader in Jiangsu Province, director of the Department of Rehabilitation Medicine, Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University. Mainly focuses on neuromuscular and bone joint rehabilitation and rehabilitation engineering, and neuroelectrophysiology.

![Wang Hongxing](https://imic.nuist.edu.cn/__local/B/C5/6C/E64FC05A79F6FF2809772F9AA76_D0E5D935_E1C2.jpg)

Hosted and participated in more than 10 national natural science and provincial and municipal level projects, and 1 China-Norway international cooperative education project. He has published 30 SCI papers and Chinese core journals as the first author or corresponding author, and is the chief editor, deputy editor, and co-editor of more than 10 monographs and textbooks. Obtained 1 invention patent and 3 software copyrights as the first author; won the third prize of the Chinese Medical Association Science and Technology Award, the first prize of the Provincial Department of Health’s Medical New Technology Introduction Award, the second prize of the Provincial Medical Science and Technology Award, and the second prize of the National Teaching Achievement Award.

He is currently the deputy director of the Electrodiagnosis Committee of the Chinese Society of Rehabilitation Medicine, the deputy director of the Rehabilitation Medicine Education Special Committee of the Chinese Society of Rehabilitation Medicine and the chairman of the Youth Committee, a standing committee member of the Youth Working Committee, a member of the Chinese Spinal Cord Injury Society of the International Spinal Cord Society, a youth committee member of the Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation Branch of the Chinese Medical Association, a member of the Neurotoxin Branch of the Chinese Society of Neuroscience, a member of the Basic Group of the Nerve Regeneration and Repair Professional Committee of the Chinese Association of Research Hospitals, a deputy director of the National Spinal Cord Injury Care Alliance, and a member of the Sports Medicine Branch of Jiangsu Medical Doctors Association, Jiangsu Province. Chairman of the Rehabilitation Medicine Special Committee of the Provincial Preventive Medicine Society, Executive Director of the Jiangsu Rehabilitation Medicine Society, Chairman of the Neuromuscular Electrophysiological Diagnosis Special Committee of the Jiangsu Rehabilitation Medicine Society and Deputy Chairman of the Rehabilitation Education Special Committee, Respiratory Rehabilitation Special Committee of the Jiangsu Rehabilitation Medicine Society and Musculoskeletal Member of the Standing Committee of the Special Committee, Vice Chairman of the Nanjing Rehabilitation Medicine Association, member of the Science Popularization Specialty Branch of the Nanjing Medical Association, corresponding editorial board member of the "Chinese Journal of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation", editorial board member of the "Chinese Journal of Cerebrovascular Disease", reviewer of the "Chinese Journal of Rehabilitation Medicine" and "Chinese Rehabilitation" magazines.
