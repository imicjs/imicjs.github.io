Wang Yuancheng, MD, deputy administrative director of the Department of Radiology, Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University, has been engaged in imaging diagnosis for more than 10 years. Wang Yuancheng, MD, deputy chief physician, has been engaged in imaging diagnosis for more than ten years. His expertise and research fields are abdominal imaging, imaging artificial intelligence and functional magnetic resonance imaging. He is a visiting scholar at the Prince of Wales Hospital in Hong Kong (2019). Hosted one youth project of the National Natural Science Foundation of China and one youth project of the Natural Science Foundation of Jiangsu Province, and was one of the “Six Talent Peaks” and “333” high-level talent training targets in Jiangsu Province.

![Wang Yuancheng](https://imic.nuist.edu.cn/__local/C/A4/4D/E6CEC1117AD75988746FC135FD0_71943BC7_9880.jpg)

He has won the third prize of the Chinese Medical Science and Technology Award (2020, 3/8), the first prize of the Jiangsu Province Medical Science and Technology Award (2019, 3/5), the second prize of the Jiangsu Province Science and Technology Award (2019, 3/9), the honorary title of Jiangsu Province Outstanding Young Radiology Physician (2019), and the first prize of the Jiangsu Province Medical New Technology Introduction Award (2017, ranked 1st). ), Chinese Medical Association Abdominal Imaging Group Outstanding Young Scholar Award (2014), etc.

He is currently a member of the Youth Committee of the Radiology Branch of the Chinese Medical Association, a member of the Youth Committee of the Chinese Association of Radiologists and a member of the Imaging Artificial Intelligence Committee, a deputy director of the Youth Committee of the Jiangsu Society of Radiology, and a member of the Abdominal Group of the Jiangsu Society of Radiology. Published more than ten SCI papers.

Host scientific research projects

1. National Natural Science Foundation of China Youth Project, 81801669, Early functional magnetic resonance evaluation of renal microcirculation disorders and fibrosis in diabetes, 2019/01-2021/12, Research in progress, Host

2. Jiangsu Provincial Natural Science Foundation Youth Project, BK20180378, Application of functional magnetic resonance in the early evaluation of diabetic renal microcirculation disorders and fibrosis, 2018/07-2021/06, Research in Progress, Host

Ongoing projects: Artificial intelligence-based research on active lesions after TACE for liver cancer (2022-2026)

Transarterial chemoembolization (TACE) is the first-line treatment for intermediate-stage liver cancer. Accurate evaluation of active lesions after TACE is key to evaluating efficacy and formulating subsequent treatment plans. At present, there are still a series of problems in the evaluation of active lesions, such as low efficiency of manual evaluation, poor accuracy of evaluation results, and limited value in guiding treatment. In recent years, image evaluation models assisted by artificial intelligence have developed rapidly and been applied in many fields. However, its use in the evaluation of active lesions after TACE is still in its infancy and has great research and clinical translation prospects. This project will achieve sensitive detection, accurate diagnosis and outcome prediction of active lesions by optimizing deep learning algorithms and combining radiomics, manual evaluation and other information.
