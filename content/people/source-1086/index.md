Ge Zheng, doctor of medicine, chief physician, professor, doctoral supervisor, and postdoctoral co-supervisor.

Currently, he is the director of the Hematology Department of Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University, the director of the Institute of Hematology of Southeast University, the director of the International Collaborating Laboratory of Hematology, the director of the Collaborative Transformation Innovation Center for Precision Diagnosis and Treatment of Malignant Hematological Diseases of Jiangsu Research Hospital Association, and the director of the Leukemia and MDS Center of Nanjing Zhongda. Main research directions: medical-engineering integration of hematological malignancies, clinical transformation, artificial intelligence precision diagnosis and treatment, medical big data, etc. He has presided over 4 projects of the MMAAP Medical Fund of the United States, 4 general projects of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, 20 provincial key research and development projects, etc., and is a young and middle-aged leading talent of Jiangsu Province’s “333 High-level Talent Project” and a key medical talent of Jiangsu Province. Research results have been published in authoritative journals in the field of hematology such as Blood and Leukemia. He serves as a member of the Hematology Branch of the Chinese Medical Doctor Association, a member of the Hematology Branch of the Chinese Society of Integrated Traditional Chinese and Western Medicine, a member of the Hematology Branch of the Chinese Society of Traditional Chinese Medicine, a member of the Hematology Branch of the Chinese Geriatrics Society, the deputy director of the Hematology Branch of the Jiangsu Provincial Medical Association and the deputy leader of the leukemia group, the vice president of the Hematology Physician Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Doctors Association, the chairman of the Leukemia and MDS Special Committee of the Jiangsu Research Hospital Association, and the deputy chairman of the Hematology Branch of the Jiangsu Geriatrics Society.
