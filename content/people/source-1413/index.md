2. Personal resume

•Tenured professor at the Department of Physics, New Academy of Sciences, NOVA University, Lisbon, and also works at the LIBPHYS-UNL Research Center

•Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering, Instituto Superior Técnico, Affiliated to the Technical University of Lisbon

•The doctoral thesis "Multimodal behavioral biometrics based on HCI and electrophysiology" proposed a new behavioral biometric model and made an important contribution to the latest technology in the field. In 2008, it won the Portuguese National Award "Futuras Promessas" ISA/Millennium BCP Award, which is awarded to the best doctoral thesis in the field of physics, electronics, information or biomedical engineering.

•IEEE Senior Member

•Former scientific committee chairman and senior scientist of the Portuguese branch of the German Fraunhofer Association, focusing on hardware design, signal processing and machine learning application research and industry technology transfer. The Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft is the largest applied scientific research institution in Germany and Europe.

•Director of Madan Parque High-tech Industry Incubator in Portugal (65 companies have been incubated)

•Founder and President of PLUX Wearable Wireless Biosignal Sensors, leading the technology innovation company's interdisciplinary team to achieve the company's research vision, covering microelectronics, biosignal processing and software development. Negotiated with national venture capital company CAIXA CAPITAL to implement the investment market development plan and introduce PLUX to the European medical device market

•In 2007, he received funding from the US-Portuguese Development Foundation and went to the Pattern Recognition and Image Processing Laboratory of Michigan State University under the guidance of internationally renowned scientist Anil Jain to conduct research.

•Served as a professor at Setubal Institute of Technology from 2000 to 2007, teaching courses in the field of artificial intelligence
