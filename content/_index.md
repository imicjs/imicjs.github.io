# Intelligence for better medicine

The **Jiangsu Key Laboratory of Intelligent Medical Image Computing (IMIC)** brings artificial intelligence, information science, engineering, and clinical medicine together to address consequential challenges in human health.

Founded in 2021 and formally approved in June 2024, IMIC Lab is directed by Professor Jun Xu. Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology is the host institution, with Zhongda Hospital Southeast University, Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital, and Jiangsu Province Hospital as co-founding clinical partners.

## Medicine, engineering, and AI—working as one

Our guiding principle is **“Inspired by the clinic, elevated beyond the clinic, empowering the clinic, and returning to the clinic.”** We pursue original research while translating computational advances into tools that can improve prevention, diagnosis, treatment planning, intervention, monitoring, and prognosis.

## Research areas

### Computational Pathology

Digital pathology, multimodal clinical records, and omics for diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment-response prediction.

### Medical Imaging & Analysis

Advanced imaging and computational methods for interpretation, planning, intervention, and disease monitoring.

### Brain Science & Neuroimaging

Brain structure, function, development, disease, neuro-inspired AI, and brain–computer interfaces.

### Multi-omics & Medical Big Data

Integrated analysis of electronic health records, genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and epigenomics.

### Physiological Signals & Wearables

Wearable sensing and multimodal analysis of electrical and mechanical physiological signals.
