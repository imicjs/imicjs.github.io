The Jiangsu Key Laboratory of Intelligent Medical Image Computing (IMIC) was founded in 2021 and formally approved for development in June 2024. Professor Jun Xu serves as Laboratory Director. Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST) is the host institution, and three major hospitals in Jiangsu—Zhongda Hospital Southeast University, Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital, and Jiangsu Province Hospital—are co-founding institutions.

IMIC works at the frontier of artificial intelligence, information science, engineering, and medicine. Our goal is to build a nationally leading and internationally recognized interdisciplinary research platform that identifies important scientific questions in medicine and health, advances fundamental methodology, and translates discoveries into clinical value.

## Research and clinical translation

We study cross-scale, multimodal data including high-resolution whole-slide pathology images, X-ray, CT, multiparametric MRI, PET, electronic health records, clinical text, multi-omics, and physiological signals. Our methods span quantitative image analysis, natural language processing, signal processing, machine learning, and multimodal artificial intelligence.

These capabilities support research in breast, liver, pancreatic, gastrointestinal, gynecologic, urologic, neurological, and ophthalmic diseases, from prevention and diagnosis to prognosis, treatment planning, intervention, and longitudinal monitoring.

## Education and team

The laboratory educates students from undergraduate through doctoral level in Medical Information Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Electronic Information with a Biomedical Engineering focus, and related fields. Engineering and clinical supervisors jointly mentor graduate students. More than 90% of the core team graduated from globally ranked universities in China, the United States, the United Kingdom, Singapore, and other countries, and most have international study or research experience.

## Facilities

IMIC operates dedicated high-performance computing infrastructure for deep learning and large-model training. Its imaging facilities include a Leica GT450 high-throughput whole-slide scanner and an in-house micrometer-resolution optical coherence tomography (μOCT) system.

## Location

The laboratory is located on the 17th floor of Linjing Building (Information Science and Technology Building), NUIST, 219 Ningliu Road, Jiangbei New Area, Nanjing, Jiangsu, China.

For taxi or private-car access, the recommended entrance is NUIST West Campus South Gate on Longshan North Road.
