Recruitment direction

The institute has long-term recruitment of outstanding master's degree students, doctoral students and postdoctoral students in the fields of artificial intelligence, biomedical engineering, computer science, automation, mathematics, statistics, bioinformatics, brain science, electronic engineering, machine learning, computer vision, image analysis, medical image computing, robotics, medical materials, medical equipment, optical instruments and other fields.

Recruitment conditions

(1) Master’s degree admission conditions

1. Pass the preliminary examination and re-examination for master’s degree at Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology;

2. Have a background in related disciplines such as artificial intelligence, biomedical engineering, computer science, automation, mathematics, statistics, bioinformatics, brain science, electronic engineering, machine learning, computer vision, image analysis, medical image computing, robotics, medical materials, medical equipment or optical instruments;

3. Have good subjective initiative, strong sense of responsibility and self-motivation, and good communication and coordination skills.

(2) PhD recruitment requirements

1. Pass the preliminary examination and re-examination for doctoral studies at Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology;

2. Have a background in related disciplines such as artificial intelligence, biomedical engineering, computer science, automation, mathematics, statistics, bioinformatics, brain science, electronic engineering, machine learning, computer vision, image analysis, medical image computing, robotics, medical materials, medical equipment or optical instruments;

3. Have good scientific research potential and comprehensive ability;

4. Have good subjective initiative, strong sense of responsibility and self-motivation, and good communication and coordination skills.

(3) Postdoctoral recruitment requirements

1. Research staff (including foreign personnel) who have obtained a doctoral degree at home or abroad; doctoral graduates who have obtained a doctoral degree no more than 3 years ago and are under the age of 35;

2. Have a background in related disciplines such as artificial intelligence, biomedical engineering, computer science, automation, mathematics, statistics, bioinformatics, brain science, electronic engineering, machine learning, computer vision, image analysis, medical image computing, robotics, medical materials, medical equipment or optical instruments;

3. Have good scientific research potential and comprehensive ability;

4. Good subjective initiative, strong sense of responsibility and self-motivation;

5. Good teamwork, communication and coordination skills, and excellent written English skills.

Postdoctoral salary

Included in the "Longshan Scholar Support Plan" of Nanjing University of Science and Technology, it provides market-competitive treatment conditions based on the relevant regulations of the school's postdoctoral management, and can assist in applying for China Postdoctoral Innovation Fund, Boxin Plan, Zhuobo Plan, "Introduction Project Plan", "Special Funding (in front of the Station)" plan related projects, etc.; the annual salary is jointly determined by the school and the cooperating tutor, with an annual salary starting from 250,000 yuan. It also helps to apply for apartments or rental subsidies, children's schooling and other generous benefits; after leaving the station, you can participate in the school's talent recruitment, and priority admission will be given under the same conditions.

For details on basic requirements and salary benefits, please refer to the "Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology High-Level Talent Recruitment Notice (2023)" (scan the QR code below or visit the website below)

Introduction to Smart Medical Research Institute

In response to the national "Healthy China" and "Digital Economy" strategies, Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology and Zhongda Hospital rely on the "Life Industry Cultivation Plan" of Nanjing Jiangbei New District and the National Health and Medical Big Data (Eastern) Center to gather global human resources and innovation elements to jointly build a first-class domestic medical-industry cross-collaborative innovation platform: the Smart Medical Research Institute. The construction of the institute embodies "the right time, the right location, and the right people." The new Jiangbei campus of Zhongda Hospital under construction is only separated from the campus of Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology. The physical distance between the institutions is very close. Therefore, it will be the best place to carry out scientific exploration, clinical translation research, and application of research results in the cutting-edge medical and engineering fields.

Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology - Zhongda Hospital Smart Medical Research Institute aims at new concepts, new technologies, and new methods of smart medical care, aiming to create a domestic first-class and internationally renowned cutting-edge and cross-cutting research platform. Professor Teng Gaojun, an academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, serves as the dean, and Professor Xu Jun serves as the executive dean. The institute has a strong faculty, including more than 20 senior medical experts including Academician Teng Gaojun, and 13 engineering experts including Professor Xu Jun. Relying on the School of Artificial Intelligence (College of Future Technology) of Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology, the average age of the institute's engineering experts is 31 years old. There are 3 senior professional titles, 1 national-level talent, and 4 provincial-level talents. 100% have overseas study experience, 62% have obtained a doctorate degree overseas, and more than 90% are ranked in the top 200 of QS in China, the United States, and the United Kingdom. They have obtained doctorate degrees from top universities. They are a high-level team with good international vision, diverse and diversified sources, young and energetic team. Supported by the intersection of medical and engineering disciplines, the institute is committed to exploring and solving major scientific problems in the frontier fields of medicine and health disciplines, promoting the in-depth integration and intersection of artificial intelligence, big data, robotics, electronic information and other information engineering and medical fields, and promoting scientific and technological innovation and achievement transformation of medical artificial intelligence. The institute actively explores the long-term mechanism of "organic integration of scientific research and teaching, mutual promotion of innovation and application, and coordinated development of universities and hospitals". Through close collaboration between schools and colleges, such as platform construction, scientific research cooperation, and achievement transformation, it promotes the deep integration of innovative resources, accelerates the development of new engineering and new medical disciplines, and cultivates comprehensive and innovative talents with a combination of science, engineering, and medicine.

The institute integrates the advantageous disciplines, high-end talents, and medical data resources of Nanjing Jiangbei New Area, Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology, and Zhongda Hospital to carry out international cutting-edge and original research in five areas: surgical robot domain navigation, medical imaging intelligent diagnosis, medical big data, medical devices and materials, and climate change and health risks.
