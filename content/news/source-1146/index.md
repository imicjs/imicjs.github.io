Roundtable

Topic:

1. Challenges and future of medical image artificial intelligence;

2. How to achieve the fusion of pathology and traditional medical images;

3. Medical-industrial intersection and federated learning;

4. Difficulties, causes and solutions faced by medical imaging AI software in clinical applications;

5. Does the field of medical image analysis require large-scale pre-trained basic models?

6. In many medical image analysis problems, it is difficult to obtain an absolute gold standard. Considering the variability and randomness of doctors’ annotations, how can the medical image analysis algorithm not be affected by annotation errors?

7. How to solve the problem that the performance of the medical image analysis algorithm may decrease when changing a center or changing a batch of data?
