On August 24, the National Natural Science Foundation of China issued the "Notice on the Review Results of the National Natural Science Foundation's Centralized Acceptance of Application Projects in 2023". Xuan Kai, Wang Xiangxue, Jiao Yiping, Zhang Teng, and Li Jin of the Smart Medical Research Institute were approved for the National Natural Science Foundation project. Recently, Wan Zhuo and Zhang Teng of the Smart Medical Research Institute were approved for the Jiangsu Provincial Natural Science Foundation Youth Project, and Wang Xiangxue was approved for the Jiangsu Provincial College Natural Science Project. Congratulations to the above teachers!
