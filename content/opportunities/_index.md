IMIC welcomes outstanding candidates from around the world. We recruit master’s and doctoral students, postdoctoral researchers, and faculty across artificial intelligence, biomedical engineering, computer science, automation, mathematics, statistics, bioinformatics, brain science, electronic engineering, machine learning, computer vision, image analysis, medical image computing, robotics, medical materials, medical devices, and optical instrumentation.

Graduate applicants must satisfy the relevant admission requirements of Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology. Prospective students and researchers are encouraged to review current notices and contact the laboratory through the official Chinese website.

[View current recruitment and admissions notices](https://imic.nuist.edu.cn/rczp_zs.htm)
