![Li Ling](/images/articles/source-1068/01.webp)

Li Ling, doctor of medicine from Germany, postdoctoral fellow at UCLA in the United States, chief physician, professor, and doctoral supervisor. Director of the Pancreatic Research Institute of Southeast University, Director of the Glucolipid Metabolism Research Center of Southeast University, Assistant to the President and Director of the Endocrinology Department of Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University. He has long been committed to research related to pancreatic endocrine and exocrine diseases and islet function damage, actively promotes the integration of medicine and engineering and innovative transformation, and carries out multi-faceted international cooperation. Published more than 50 papers in SCI journals such as Nature Communications, including 5 high-impact papers with scores of 10 or above. He serves as a communication reviewer for the Department of Medical Sciences of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, a doctoral dissertation review expert for the Ministry of Education, and a guest editor of SCI cutting-edge journals; a youth committee member of the Diabetes Branch of the Chinese Medical Association, a deputy chairman of the Diabetes Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Association, and a deputy chairman of the Endocrinology Branch of the Nanjing Medical Association.

Hosted projects: 1 national key R&D plan sub-project, 5 National Natural Science Foundation projects, 1 Jiangsu Provincial key R&D plan project, and 1 Jiangsu Provincial Health Commission key project. Based on research results, we actively promote cutting-edge research on inter-organ tissue dialogue between diabetes and pancreatic diseases, conduct international multi-center clinical trials, and apply big data in medical systems, information platform construction, health monitoring and clinical decision-making assistance.

Main awards and talent titles: Won the National Ministry of Education’s Higher Education Institutions Scientific Research Outstanding Achievements Award, Jiangsu Province Youth Science and Technology Award, Jiangsu Medical Science and Technology Award, and Jiangsu Province New Technology Introduction Award. Jiangsu Province’s “333 High-level Talent Training Project” (second level, leading talents), Jiangsu Province’s “Six Talent Peaks”, Jiangsu Province’s “Science, Education and Health” key medical talents, Jiangsu Province’s “Six One Project” high-level talents.
