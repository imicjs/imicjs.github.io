Chai Renjie, chief professor and second-level professor of Southeast University, executive dean of the Institute of Advanced Life and Health, and deputy dean of the School of Life Sciences and Technology of Southeast University; has been selected into 9 talent plans including ****, chief scientist of the National Key R&D Program of the Ministry of Science and Technology, Youth Thousand Talents, and National Outstanding Youth. Main research direction: Study on the multi-gene coordinated regulation mechanism of endogenous stem cell regeneration of hair cells and neurons.

In charge of the Ministry of Education **** Distinguished Professor, Chief Scientist of the National Key R&D Program of the Ministry of Science and Technology, Youth of the Organization Department of the Central Committee **** , National Outstanding Youth Science Fund, Ministry of Education Fok Ying-tung Educational Foundation Outstanding Young Teacher Fund, Jiangsu Provincial Outstanding Youth Fund, Jiangsu Provincial Entrepreneurship Talents, Jiangsu Provincial Entrepreneurship Team, Jiangsu Province 333 Engineering Talents, Jiangsu Province Six Talent Peak High-level Talents and other talent plans; has served as a project or subject leader in charge of the Ministry of Science and Technology’s National Key R&D Program Stem Cell and Transformation Research Key Special Project, the Ministry of Science and Technology’s Youth 973 Program, China Science and Technology The college's "Organ Reconstruction and Manufacturing" Class A strategic leading science and technology project, Military Brain Science Project and Foundation Strengthening Plan key basic research project of the Military Commission Science and Technology Commission, 1 National Natural Science Foundation of China key project, 1 major integrated project, 2 general projects, 1 youth project, Jiangsu Provincial Key R&D Plan Social Development Project, Sichuan Provincial Science and Technology Plan Key R&D Project, Jiangsu Provincial Natural Science Foundation of China Youth Project and other 29 projects. In the past five years, he has published 125 high-quality papers in three categories in first-class academic journals such as Cell as the corresponding author. 4 papers were listed as recommended papers by Faculty1000, 1 paper was selected as the best paper of the year in the regular issue of Cell, 13 papers were selected as ESI highly cited papers, 2 papers were selected as hot papers, with a total of more than 6,000 citations, h-index 48; As chief editor, he published 3 monographs, applied for 23 national invention patents, and authorized 5 national invention patents and 1 utility model patent.

He is currently the President of the Hearing, Speech and Communication Branch of the Chinese Biophysical Society; Vice Chairman and Chairman-elect of the Stem Cell Physiology Committee of the Chinese Physiological Society; Chairman of the Basic Research Expert Committee and Standing Member of the Expert Steering Committee of the China Hearing Medicine Development Foundation; Executive Board Member of the International Association of Otologists (Executive board Member); Vice Chairman of the Otology Branch of the China Association for Medical Promotion and Leader of the Hearing Basic Research Group; Chairman of the Medical Device Branch of the China Biomedicine Industry Chain Innovation and Transformation Alliance. His research results have won 9 academic awards and scientific and technological awards, including the National Innovation Award, the First Prize of the Huo Yingdong Youth Science Award, the China Youth Science and Technology Award, the Shulan Medical Youth Award, and the First Prize of the Natural Science Award of the Ministry of Education.
