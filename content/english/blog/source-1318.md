Zhang Bing, chief physician, professor, doctoral supervisor of Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital, MD, postdoctoral fellow at Mayo Clinic, Outstanding Youth of the Chinese Radiology Association; a famous doctor in the country with outstanding style; a high-level talent in Jiangsu Province. Currently, he is deputy secretary of the Party Committee and director of the Medical Imaging Center of the Drum Tower Hospital Affiliated to Nanjing University School of Medicine; deputy director of the Institute of Brain Science of Nanjing University; director of the Institute of Imaging and Artificial Intelligence of Nanjing University; member of the Radiologist Branch of the Chinese Medical Doctor Association and deputy leader of the neuroimaging group; deputy director and secretary-general of the Medical Imaging Center Branch of the Chinese Hospital Association; Jiang Vice President of the Jiangsu Medical Association; Vice President of the Radiologist Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Association; Vice Chairman of the Stroke Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Association and leader of the Cerebrovascular Imaging Group; Member of the Standing Committee of the Radiology Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Association and leader of the Quality Intelligence Group; Chairman of the Radiology Branch of the Nanjing Medical Association; Director of the Nanjing Branch Center of the Radiation Diagnosis Quality Control Center of Jiangsu Province.

Professor Zhang Bing is the subject leader of the "Brain Science" major project of the Ministry of Science and Technology. He has presided over 6 National Natural Science Foundation projects (2 key projects, including Key 1 - the National "Belt and Road" Demonstration Project), won 3 provincial first prizes (First Prize in Jiangsu Medical Science and Technology), and published 110 first/communication SCI papers: The main results were published in top journals such as Radiology, Diabetes Care, Neuroimage, etc., including 33 SCI area 1 papers, and the highest impact factor of published papers is 29.146. Professor Zhang Bing pioneered olfactory and spatial navigation fMRI methods, and has 14 authorized patents, 15 software copyrights, and 5 domestic and foreign standards/consensus.
