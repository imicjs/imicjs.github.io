Ju Shenghong, Doctor of Medicine, is chief professor, chief physician and doctoral supervisor of Southeast University. He is currently the deputy dean of the School of Medicine of Southeast University and the director of the Imaging Department of the Affiliated Zhongda Hospital. He is a winner of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars, a national “****” leading scientific and technological innovation leader, and the head of the “Key Field Innovation Team” of the Ministry of Science and Technology.

The main research directions include imaging diagnosis, molecular imaging, artificial intelligence and radiomics. He has presided over more than 10 national-level projects such as national key research and development plan projects, key projects of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and key projects of major research plans. Published more than 100 papers as the first author/corresponding author. He has won more than 10 awards, including the second prize of the National Science and Technology Progress Award, the second prize of the National Teaching Achievement Award, the first prize of the Ministry of Education’s Science and Technology Progress Award, and the second prize of the Jiangsu Province Science and Technology Progress Award.

He is currently a member of the Standing Committee of the Chinese Society of Radiology, a member of the Standing Committee of the Chinese Association of Radiologists, a member of the Medical Technology Teaching Steering Committee of Higher Education Institutions of the Ministry of Education, a deputy chairman of the Jiangsu Provincial Society of Radiology, and an executive committee member of the Asian Abdominal Imaging Society. Won the annual gold medal of the Chinese Society of Radiology, the national “Good Teacher among Resident Physicians”, the fifth batch of “333 High-level Talents” of Jiangsu Province, the Outstanding Educator of Jiangsu Province, and the “Six Talent Peaks” of Jiangsu Province.
