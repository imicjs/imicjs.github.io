In order to help students gain an in-depth understanding of the frontiers of scientific research and guide students to further clarify their goals and efforts in the university, on the morning of March 16, the School of Artificial Intelligence (College of Future Technology) organized some members of the smart medical community and student representatives of all grades to go to the Jiangbei New District Huichuang Park Base of the Smart Medical Research Institute of Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology to carry out visits and face-to-face exchanges between teachers and students.

![The School of Artificial Intelligence organizes students to visit the Smart Medical Research Institute](/images/articles/source-1174/01.webp)

Students participating in the event took a group photo with teachers from the Smart Medical Research Institute

Professor Xu Jun, deputy dean of the School of Artificial Intelligence (College of Future Technology) and executive director of the Smart Medical Research Institute, first introduced the construction background, concepts, goals and research fields of the Smart Medical Research Institute to the students in detail. He introduced the broad development prospects of the Smart Medical Research Institute through industrial background, geographical location, faculty, development opportunities and trends, analyzed the many major challenges that humans still face in the health field, and pointed out the importance and potential clinical value of artificial intelligence technology in empowering the medical field. He encouraged students to actively think and work hard to cultivate their fields and directions of interest, and set their own goals for the future. He hoped that students could study hard and actively participate in scientific research in the field of smart medical care in the future.

The students visited the Smart Medical Research Institute under the leadership of Dean Xu Jun and deeply felt the strong academic atmosphere of the institute. The engineering teachers of the Smart Medical Research Institute also showed the cutting-edge research fields being conducted by the institute to the visiting students, and shared a number of exciting latest research results. During the interactive session, teachers and students had in-depth communication and exchanges, and the atmosphere was warm and harmonious.

The School of Artificial Intelligence (College of Future Technology) actively implements the fundamental task of cultivating people with moral integrity and attaches great importance to talent cultivation. Based on the condensation and construction of the three major research institutes of the college, it has established three student societies: Yuan Universe, Smart Meteorology and Smart Medical. It is committed to cultivating students' innovative and entrepreneurial thinking and improving students' innovative and entrepreneurial abilities. The smooth implementation of this activity is conducive to guiding and motivating students to actively participate in scientific and technological innovation practice, show their youthful style, and play a positive role in promoting the formation of the college's excellent academic style and mass entrepreneurship and innovation cultural atmosphere.
