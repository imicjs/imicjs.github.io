From May 17th to 18th, the 2024 Smart Medical and Health Summit Forum and Medical Image Computing Youth Symposium (MICS) offline academic exchange event was held at Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology. This event is the sixth Science and Technology Activity Month of Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology and the 64th anniversary celebration of Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology. It is also a sub-forum of the Development Conference of the School of Artificial Intelligence (College of Future Technology) of Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology and the Metaverse and Artificial Intelligence Application Innovation Forum.

Xu Jun, chairman of the 4th MICS Committee and deputy dean of the School of Artificial Intelligence of Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology, presided over the speech. Academician Teng Gaojun of Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University, the honorary chairman of this forum, delivered an opening speech via video. Ten Yangtze River and outstanding young people from the field of smart medical and health in my country gathered in Fengyun Theater of our school to give wonderful sharings. The invited speakers of this forum include National Distinguished Youth Professor Li Lihua from Hangzhou University of Electronic Science and Technology, National Distinguished Youth Professor Liu Huafeng from Zhejiang University, National Distinguished Youth Professor Li Min from Central South University, National Distinguished Youth Professor Li Guoqi from the Institute of Automation of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, National Distinguished Youth Professor Cai Hongmin from South China University of Technology, Yangtze Scholar Professor Luo Jianwen from Tsinghua University, National Distinguished Youth Professor Li Yang from Beijing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, MICS initiator and IEEE Fellow. , the honorary chairmen of this forum are Professor Shen Dinggang of ShanghaiTech University, Yangtze River Scholar Professor Chen Xun of University of Science and Technology of China, and Professor Zhang Yudong of Southeast University. The meeting was hosted by 10 host experts, including Professor Zhang Daoqiang of Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Professor He Huiguang of the Institute of Automation of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Professor Yang Xin of the National Excellent Youth University of Science and Technology, Professor Liu Yong of the Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Professor Zhao Shijie of Northwestern Polytechnical University, Professor Shi Jun of Shanghai University, Professor Wang Liansheng of Xiamen University, Professor Lei Boying of the National Excellent Youth Shenzhen University, Professor Zhao Yitian of the Ningbo Institute of Materials Technology and Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and Professor Yang Guanyu of Southeast University.

This forum is quite influential, attracting more than 300 participants from my country's university teachers, researchers from research institutions, hospital doctors and researchers from enterprises and institutions. Participants came from 27 provinces, municipalities, autonomous regions and special administrative regions, 45 large, medium and small cities, and 138 universities, hospitals, research institutions and enterprises and institutions. Participants were divided according to research fields, including university teachers, senior doctors, senior executives of well-known companies and authoritative experts from research institutions in the two major fields of medicine and engineering, including medical imaging, pathology, brain and neuroscience, pharmacy, pediatrics, ophthalmology and medical artificial intelligence. The number of online participants reached nearly 20,000, which fully demonstrated the huge development potential of the medical-engineering intersection and multi-disciplinary integration fields involved in this forum. This forum not only highlights the trend of deep integration of medicine and engineering, but will also effectively promote the close integration and mutual penetration of information engineering and medical fields such as artificial intelligence, big data, robotics, electronic information, automation, and computers, and inject strong impetus into the scientific and technological innovation and achievement transformation of medical artificial intelligence in my country. The schedule of this forum is reasonable, and the 10 forum special reports are brilliant. There are sufficient questions and discussion sessions at the end of each report. The forum has been well received by both offline and online audiences. Finally, Professor Xu Jun, executive chairman of this forum, made a summary.
