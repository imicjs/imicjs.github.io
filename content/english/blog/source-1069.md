Ma Genshan, male, doctor of medicine, second-level professor, chief physician, doctoral supervisor, director of the Department of Cardiovascular Medicine, Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University, and deputy director of the Institute of Cardiovascular Diseases of Southeast University. He has been engaged in interventional cardiology since 1989. He studied in Australia and is good at the interventional diagnosis and treatment of various cardiovascular diseases. In particular, the interventional treatment technology of coronary heart disease enjoys a high reputation in China. He has independently completed and guided more than 10,000 cases of coronary artery balloon angioplasty and stent surgery, and organized and chaired 19 international academic conferences on coronary heart disease revascularization.

He is currently the Chairman-elect of the Cardiovascular Disease Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Association, the Chairman of the Cardiovascular Medicine Branch of the Nanjing Medical Association, the Vice President of the Cardiovascular Physicians Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Doctors Association, the Interventional Cardiology Group Member of the Cardiovascular Branch of the Chinese Medical Association, the Standing Committee of the Cardiovascular Physicians Branch of the Chinese Medical Doctors Association, and the Cardiovascular Physicians Branch of the Chinese Medical Doctors Association. Deputy Chairman of the Heart Failure Professional Committee, Deputy Chairman of the Echocardiography and Imaging Group of the Cardiovascular Physicians Branch Committee of the Chinese Medical Doctor Association, Deputy Chairman of the ISHR Chinese Translational Medicine Working Committee, Member of the Executive Committee of the China Chest Pain Center, Standing Member of the Cardiac Rehabilitation Management Professional Committee of the Chinese Hospital Association, and Physician of Jiangsu Province Vice Chairman of the Hypertension Professional Committee of the Association, Vice Chairman of the Cardiovascular Inflammation and Immunity Professional Committee of the Jiangsu Society of Immunology, Leader of the Heart Failure Group of the Cardiovascular Disease Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Association, and Member of the American College of Cardiology; Leader of key medical disciplines in Jiangsu Province, Outstanding Key Talents of Jiangsu Province, and Leading Medical Talents of Jiangsu Province ; Member of the National Natural Science Foundation of China peer review expert group, National Development and Reform Commission Drug Pricing Committee, National Ministry of Education international cooperation project evaluation expert; "Chinese Journal of Cardiovascular Diseases", "Chinese Journal of Heart Failure and Cardiomyopathy", "Chinese Journal of Interventional Cardiology", "Chinese Journal of Molecular Cardiology" and other medical journals.

He has completed and undertaken 6 National Natural Science Foundation projects, 1 key R&D plan of the Jiangsu Provincial Department of Science and Technology, 11 provincial and ministerial level projects, won 1 second prize and 5 third prizes of the Jiangsu Provincial Science and Technology Award, 12 New Technology Introduction Awards from the Jiangsu Provincial Department of Health, and obtained 2 invention patents. He has trained and mentored 27 doctoral students and 58 master's students. He has published more than 180 papers in core journals at home and abroad, and has edited or co-edited 10 monographs.
