Yuan Yonggui, MD, chief physician, young distinguished professor, doctoral supervisor, outstanding key medical talent in Jiangsu Province, second-level training target of the fifth phase of Jiangsu Province’s “333 Project”, leader of the psychiatry innovation team of Jiangsu Province’s “Strengthening Health through Science and Education”, director of the Department of Psychology and Psychiatry, Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University, member of the International Council of Psychosomatic Medicine (ICPM), and visiting scholar at the Department of Psychiatry at the University of Florida. He has been engaged in research on the clinical diagnosis, treatment and pathogenesis of depression, the clinical diagnosis, treatment and pathogenesis of psychosomatic-related disorders, and the mechanisms of psychological assessment and psychotherapy for nearly thirty years.

He is currently the chairman of the Psychosomatic Medicine Branch of the Chinese Medical Association, the elected chairman of the Psychiatry Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Association, the chairman of the Psychosomatic Medicine Professional Committee of the Jiangsu Medical Association, the former chairman of the Psychosomatic and Behavioral Medicine Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Association, and the vice president of the Psychiatry Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Doctor Association. In 2019, he was named the "Expert with Outstanding Contributions to Psychosomatic Medicine" by the Psychosomatic Medicine Branch of the Chinese Medical Association and the title of "Excellent Chairman" by the Jiangsu Medical Association.

Hosted 6 National Natural Science Foundation of China projects (30970814, 81071101, 81371488, 81571330, 81771480 and 81971277). He has won 1 first prize and 1 third prize of the China Medical Science and Technology Award, 2 first prizes and 1 second prize of the Natural Science Award of the Ministry of Education, 1 second prize of the Huaxia Medical Science and Technology Award, 1 first prize and 1 third prize of the Jiangsu Provincial Science and Technology Award, 4 first prizes and 1 second prize of the New Technology Introduction Award of the Provincial Department of Health, and 2 second prizes and 2 third prizes of the Municipal Science and Technology Progress Award. He has edited 25 books and published 415 papers, including 187 SCI papers.
