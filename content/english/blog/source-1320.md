Chen Jiu, Doctor of Medicine, visiting scholar in the United States, professor, researcher, industrial professor of Jiangsu Province, doctoral supervisor, and postdoctoral cooperative supervisor. Deputy director of the Institute of Medical Imaging and Artificial Intelligence of Nanjing University, executive director of the Nanjing University-Gulou Hospital Intelligent Imaging Research Institute, and executive director of the Nanjing Applied Mathematics Center-Drum Tower Hospital Medical Imaging Joint Laboratory. Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital's "High-level Talent Introduction", Jiangsu Province's "333 High-level Talents" training target, Nanjing City's "Outstanding Youth Fund Project" winner, and the Ministry of Education's Degree Center Review Expert. He is a member of the Medical Artificial Intelligence Professional Committee of the Chinese Hospital Association and a member of the Dementia Disease Branch of the China Association for the Promotion of International Healthcare Exchange. Hosted more than 10 projects at national natural science, provincial and ministerial levels. Currently engaged in neuroimaging, artificial intelligence, and personalized precision intervention for the AD disease spectrum (AD, MCI, SCD). Published more than 50 SCI papers as the first/corresponding author (including co-authors). He serves as deputy editor-in-chief of “Journal of Alzheimer’s Disease” and deputy editor-in-chief of one monograph. He has won the second prize of the 2023 "Science and Technology Jiangsu" Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition, the first prize of Jiangsu Province New Technology Introduction in 2022, and more than 10 other awards.
