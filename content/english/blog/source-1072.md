Wu Guoqiu, Ph.D., professor, chief technician, doctoral supervisor. Director of the Laboratory Department of Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University, Director of the Department of Laboratory Medicine of Southeast University School of Medicine, and Director of the Institute of Clinical Drug Biotechnology of Southeast University. He is also a member of the Standing Committee of the Tumor Immunity and Companion Diagnostics Professional Committee of the Chinese Anti-Cancer Association, the Standing Committee of the POCT Technical Branch of the China Medical Equipment Association, the Standing Committee of the China Integrated Traditional Chinese and Western Medicine Tumor Immunity Professional Committee, the Deputy Chairman of the Clinical Laboratory Special Committee of the Jiangsu Provincial Hospital Association, the Deputy Chairman of the In-Vitro Diagnostic Technology Professional Committee of the Jiangsu Biomedical Engineering Society, the Molecular Diagnostic Professional Group Member of the Laboratory Branch of the Jiangsu Medical Association, and the Deputy Chairman of the Hospital Biosafety Management Special Committee of the Nanjing Hospital Association.

Evaluation expert of the National Natural Science Foundation, and has won honorary titles such as "Key Medical Talents of Jiangsu Province", "Six Peak Talents of Jiangsu Province", and "High-Level Talents of Jiangsu Province 333 Project"; he has presided over 14 projects including major national science and technology projects, National Natural Science Foundation, and key science and technology R&D projects of Jiangsu Province. He has won 1 first prize for the introduction of new technologies in Jiangsu Province and 2 second prizes for medical science and technology in Jiangsu Province. He has edited 1 monograph and participated in the editing of 2 Department, has applied for 19 patents and been granted 10 patents; in recent years, he has published more than 70 SCI papers as the first or corresponding author.
