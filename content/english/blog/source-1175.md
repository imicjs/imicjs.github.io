Subsequently, the institute team conducted academic exchanges with the team of Director Zhou Zhengrong of the Department of Radiation Diagnosis of Fudan Cancer Hospital. Department of Radiation Diagnosis Dr. Xie Tiansong and Dr. Liu Wei gave academic reports on "Progress in Imaging Diagnosis of Neuroendocrine Tumor Liver Metastasis" and "Progress in Imaging Research of Pancreatic Cancer" respectively. They introduced the current research progress of neuroendocrine tumor liver metastasis and pancreatic cancer to the engineering team of the Smart Medical Research Institute, and raised scientific issues of clinical concern. The two parties had a heated discussion on how to use advanced artificial intelligence technology to solve clinical concerns and formulated plans for further cooperation.

Since the establishment of the Smart Medical Research Institute, with the care and support of school leaders and sister units, it has actively expanded outward, communicated and cooperated, and is committed to promoting innovative research and application of new artificial intelligence technologies in clinical work. Through this communication and exchange, the engineering team of the Smart Medical Research Institute has a further in-depth understanding of the clinical problems, needs and challenges of the hospital. At the same time, by sharing the latest research results and solutions of the Smart Medical Research Institute with clinicians, the mutual understanding between the two parties can be strengthened to further condense the direction of future cooperation. This exchange event strengthened communication and cooperation between the Smart Medical Research Institute and top domestic hospitals, and will help promote the application of artificial intelligence technology in the medical field in the future.
