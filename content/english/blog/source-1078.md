Xie Jianfeng, deputy chief physician, doctoral supervisor, young chief professor of Southeast University, assistant director of the Department of Critical Care Medicine, Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University. National top-notch young talents, Jiangsu Province 333 Project third-level talents, Jiangsu Province Young Medical Talents, visiting scholar at Emory University in the United States. Currently, he serves as the deputy leader of the youth group of the Society for Prevention and Treatment of Bacterial Infection and Drug Resistance of the Chinese Medical Association, a youth member of the Critical Disease Committee of the Chinese Pathophysiology Society, a member of the Critical Care Medicine Branch of the Jiangsu Provincial Medical Association, and a member of the expert database of the Emergency Office of the National Health Commission.

![Xie Jianfeng](https://imic.nuist.edu.cn/__local/5/98/7E/ABA1371A8B255A581B0A8F086C6_C4F5E5FA_8501.jpg)

He has long been engaged in basic and clinical research on Sepsis immune-inflammatory disorders. He has presided over major national R&D program projects, National Natural Science Foundation Youth Projects and general projects. He has participated as a backbone in many national medium-sized university R&D programs, National Natural Science Foundation of China and other projects. In recent years, he has published more than 80 papers in domestic and foreign magazines, including 24 SCI papers as the first author or corresponding author. Some papers were published in "Lancet Digital Health", "Intensive Care Med", "Clin Infect Dis", "Crit Care Med", "Chest", " Crit Care", "JAMA Network Open" and other authoritative professional journals.

Main honors: won the second prize of the Chinese Medical Science and Technology Progress Award (2017), the second prize of the Jiangsu Province Science and Technology Progress Award (2018), the Jiangsu Province New Technology Introduction Award (2018), and the Youth Research Award for Critical Care Medicine (2017). Hua Xia Medical Award (2020), Jiangsu Science and Technology Award (2021). Honored doctors in 2020 and advanced individuals in Jiangsu Province’s fight against the epidemic. "Anti-epidemic Hero" and "Anti-epidemic Academic Pioneer" of the Critical Care Medicine Branch of the Chinese Medical Association.
