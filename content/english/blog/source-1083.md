![Qi Xiaolong](/images/articles/source-1083/01.webp)

Qi Xiaolong, professor, doctoral supervisor, selected into the National High-Level Talent Program, assistant to the president of Zhongda Hospital Affiliated to Southeast University, director of the Center for Portal Hypertension; founder of CHESS, chairman of the alliance; founder of PH&C, editor-in-chief of the journal; chairman of the Portal Hypertension Alliance of the National Center for Infectious Diseases; Chairman of the Portal Hypertension Professional Committee of the Chinese Association for the Promotion of Human Health Science and Technology; Vice Chairman of the Portal Hypertension Outreach Committee of the American Society for the Study of the Liver.

He has long been committed to the research, development and transformation of new technologies for the diagnosis and treatment of portal hypertension in liver cirrhosis. A total of 168 SCI papers have been published, with an impact factor of 1950.849 points, 6384 citations, and H-index 41 (data updated on June 1, 2023). Main corresponding author papers: Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol (45.042), J Hepatol (30.083), Gut (31.793), Radiology (29.146), Cell Rep Med (16.988), Ann Surg (13.787), Clin Gastroenterol Hepatol (13.576), Am J Gastroenterol (12.045) .

He took the lead in formulating 3 consensus guidelines including the "China Expert Consensus on the Clinical Application of Hepatic Venous Pressure Gradient" and won the Asia-Pacific Association for the Study of the Liver (APASL) Rising Star Award (the only award-winning expert in China), DAMO Academy's "Green Orange Outstanding Finalist Award", and 2 first prizes in provincial and ministerial level Science and Technology Progress Awards.
