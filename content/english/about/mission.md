IMIC identifies important scientific questions in medicine and health, develops original computational methods, and translates research discoveries into clinical value.

> Inspired by the clinic, elevated beyond the clinic, empowering the clinic, and returning to the clinic.

## What we study

Our work spans high-resolution whole-slide pathology, X-ray, CT, multiparametric MRI, PET, electronic health records, clinical text, multi-omics, and multimodal physiological signals.

## How we work

The laboratory brings together engineering researchers, clinical specialists, and students. Its methods include quantitative image analysis, natural language processing, signal processing, machine learning, multimodal artificial intelligence, and medical-device development.

## Clinical partnerships

NUIST is the host institution. Zhongda Hospital Southeast University, Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital, and Jiangsu Province Hospital are co-founding clinical partners.
