The Nanjing edition of BIOSTEC 2027 will be held at the **Nanjing Yangzi River Conference Center Longshan Lake Hotel**.

Located in the Biopharmaceutical Valley area of Nanjing Jiangbei New Area, the hotel is close to Xinghuo Road Station on Metro Line 3 and lies beside the Longwangshan scenic area. Its conference facilities and convenient transport connections make it a practical base for delegates attending the meeting.

## Address

**Nanjing Yangzi River Conference Center Longshan Lake Hotel**  
7 Yaogu Avenue, Pukou District  
Nanjing, Jiangsu 210000  
China
