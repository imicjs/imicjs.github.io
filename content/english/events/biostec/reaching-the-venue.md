BIOSTEC 2027 will take place at the **Nanjing Yangzi River Conference Center Longshan Lake Hotel**, 7 Yaogu Avenue, Pukou District, Nanjing.

The routes and journey times below are indicative. Delegates should check a live navigation or public-transport service before travelling.

## From Nanjing Lukou International Airport

### By metro and local transfer

1. Take Metro Line S1 (Airport Line) towards Nanjing South Railway Station.
2. At Nanjing South Railway Station, change to Metro Line 3 towards Linchang.
3. Leave Xinghuo Road Station through Exit 2B.
4. Take bus 648 towards the Yangzi Petrochemical terminus and alight at **Biopharmaceutical Valley East** (生物医药谷东), then walk approximately 400 metres to the hotel. Alternatively, take a taxi or ride-hailing car from Xinghuo Road Station; the hotel is approximately 3 km away.

### By taxi or ride-hailing car

The road distance is approximately 68 km. The journey normally takes 60–75 minutes, depending on traffic.

## From Nanjing South Railway Station

### By metro and local transfer

Take Metro Line 3 towards Linchang, leave Xinghuo Road Station through Exit 2B, and continue by bus 648 to **Biopharmaceutical Valley East** or by taxi or ride-hailing car for the final 3 km.

### By taxi or ride-hailing car

The road distance is approximately 38 km and the journey normally takes around 45 minutes, depending on traffic.

## From Nanjing Railway Station

### By metro and local transfer

Take Metro Line 3 towards Linchang, leave Xinghuo Road Station through Exit 2B, and continue by bus 648 to **Biopharmaceutical Valley East** or by taxi or ride-hailing car for the final 3 km.

### By taxi or ride-hailing car

The road distance is approximately 20 km and the journey normally takes around 30 minutes, depending on traffic.

## Driving and navigation

Search for **南京扬子江国际会议中心龙山湖酒店** in your preferred map application, or enter **7 Yaogu Avenue, Pukou District, Nanjing**. Parking arrangements and any conference shuttle services will be announced separately if available.

Metro services, bus routes, and road conditions may change. Allow additional time, particularly during peak travel periods.
