Nanjing is a major transport hub in eastern China and is readily accessible by air and rail. After arriving in the city, delegates can continue to the conference hotel using the guidance on the [Getting to the Conference Venue](../reaching-the-venue/) page.

## Arriving by air

**Nanjing Lukou International Airport (NKG)** is the city's principal passenger airport. It serves domestic routes as well as selected international and regional services.

Metro Line S1 connects the airport with Nanjing South Railway Station, where passengers can transfer to other lines on the Nanjing Metro network. Taxis and app-based ride-hailing services are also available.

[Visit the Nanjing Lukou International Airport website](https://www.njiairport.com/)

Flight schedules, terminal information, baggage rules, and entry requirements should be confirmed with the airline and airport before departure.

## Arriving by rail

Nanjing's principal passenger stations are **Nanjing South Railway Station** and **Nanjing Railway Station**. Check the arrival station shown on your ticket carefully, as the two stations are in different parts of the city.

### Nanjing South Railway Station

Nanjing South is the city's main high-speed rail hub. It connects with Metro Lines 1, 3, S1, and S3.

### Nanjing Railway Station

Nanjing Railway Station serves both high-speed and conventional trains and connects with Metro Lines 1 and 3.

[Search rail services and purchase tickets through China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/)

## Travel planning

Air, rail, and local transport services can change because of weather, passenger demand, or operational requirements. Book early where possible and check current information with the airline, airport, China Railway 12306, and a live map application before travelling.
