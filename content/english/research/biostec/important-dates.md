All deadlines are stated in **Anywhere on Earth (AoE)** time.

## Joint conference papers

| Submission category | Paper submission | Author notification | Camera-ready paper and registration |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Regular paper | 15 September 2026 | 13 November 2026 | 27 November 2026 |
| Position paper / regular paper | 22 October 2026 | 4 December 2026 | 18 December 2026 |

| Milestone | Deadline |
|---|---:|

## Registration deadlines

| Registration category | Early deadline |
|---|---:|
| Conference regular-paper presenter | 27 November 2026 |
| Conference position-/regular-paper presenter | 18 December 2026 |
| Workshop presenter | 8 January 2027 |
| Special-session presenter, subject to organiser confirmation | 8 January 2027 |
| Doctoral Consortium participant | 20 January 2027 |
| Non-presenter | 20 January 2027 |

The late-registration deadline for non-presenters is **4 February 2027**. Registration may remain possible after this date, but conference bags and proceedings will be supplied only while stocks last.
