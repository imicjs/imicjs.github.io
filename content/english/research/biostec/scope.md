BIOSTEC provides a shared scientific forum for theoretical advances, validated methods, clinical translation, and practical applications across biomedical engineering systems and technologies.

<div class="conference-track-grid">
  <article class="conference-track"><h3>BIODEVICES</h3><strong>Biomedical Electronics and Devices</strong><p>Medical electronics, wearable health technologies, biosensors, medical robotics, intelligent diagnostic devices, and human–machine interaction.</p></article>
  <article class="conference-track"><h3>BIOIMAGING</h3><strong>Bioimaging Technologies and Applications</strong><p>MRI, CT, ultrasound and other imaging modalities; image processing and reconstruction; medical AI; computer-aided diagnosis; and three-dimensional medical visualisation.</p></article>
  <article class="conference-track"><h3>BIOINFORMATICS</h3><strong>Bioinformatics Methods and Applications</strong><p>Biological data analysis, genomics, proteomics, computational biology, machine learning, and data-driven methods for precision medicine.</p></article>
  <article class="conference-track"><h3>BIOSIGNALS</h3><strong>Biomedical Signal Processing</strong><p>ECG and EEG analysis, physiological signal processing, neural engineering, pattern recognition, and intelligent biosignal interpretation.</p></article>
  <article class="conference-track"><h3>HEALTHINF</h3><strong>Health Informatics</strong><p>Health-data management, electronic health records, healthcare AI, smart hospitals, clinical decision support, digital health, and intelligent care delivery.</p></article>
</div>

## An interdisciplinary research community

The conference is designed for researchers and practitioners who use information systems, artificial intelligence, signal processing, electronics, and other engineering methods to address consequential problems in biology, medicine, and healthcare. Contributions should make their technical or scientific novelty explicit and explain the biomedical setting in which the work applies.
